\section{Grandma's 100th Birthday}

Your grandma made it to 100 years old! To celebrate this amazing accomplishment,
your dad went to the farmers market to buy grandma a bunch of cakes for her party.
He picked up N long rectangular cakes, each with varying lengths. \newline \newline

Every person at the party, to be fair, will receive exactly one slice, and all
cake sizes need to be exactly the same length. You are tasked to create all the 
fair slices. You are not allowed to combine any pieces of different cakes 
together. Any leftover cake is thrown away. \newline \newline

There is one major problem though. Your dad doesn't have a knife, so he went and
bought one at the gas station and its plastic and cheap. This knife is fragile 
and will snap after exactly M cuts. \newline \newline

If it is impossible to give everyone one equal slice, return 0 \newline \newline

\subsection{Input}

The input consists of two separate lines of data, and the second being the cake sizes:
\begin{itemize}
    \item An integer $N$, the number of cakes
    \item An integer $K$, the number of people coming to the party
    \item An integer $M$, the maximum amount of cuts the knife can make
    \item The second line has $N$ space separated integers c1, c2, c3, ... , cn 
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Output}

Print a single integer of the maximum size of the slice which allows every
family member to have one equal slice, Return 0 if its impossible.

        \hline
        1 10 5 & 0\\

    \end{tabular}
\end{center}
